# Economy And Business

How money and work move through Cameroon.

This folder is organised into the subfolders below. Each subfolder has its own guide explaining what belongs in it.

- `national-statistics`: Published economic and demographic statistics, with source and date.
- `trade-and-industry`: Exports, imports, key industries and companies.
- `banking-and-mobile-money`: Financial services, mobile money and how they are used.
- `taxation-and-customs`: Tax and customs rules in plain language.
- `jobs-and-labour`: Occupations, labour rules and recruitment practices.
- `entrepreneurship`: Starting and running a business in Cameroon.
