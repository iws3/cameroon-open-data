# Culture

The traditions, arts and customs of Cameroon's peoples.

This folder is organised into the subfolders below. Each subfolder has its own guide explaining what belongs in it.

- `proverbs-and-sayings`: Proverbs and sayings in any Cameroonian language, with their meaning.
- `folktales-and-oral-literature`: Folktales, legends, riddles and other oral literature.
- `music-and-dance`: Descriptions of traditional and modern music and dance, and lyrics you have the right to share.
- `festivals-and-ceremonies`: Festivals, rites of passage, weddings, funerals and other ceremonies.
- `food-and-cuisine`: Dishes, recipes, ingredients and food customs by region and community.
- `art-and-craft`: Sculpture, weaving, pottery, beadwork and other crafts, with their makers' communities.
- `chieftaincy-and-traditions`: Traditional rulership, titles, palaces and customary institutions.
- `dress-and-attire`: Traditional and modern dress and the occasions it belongs to.
