# Daily Life

The ordinary conversations and situations of living in Cameroon.

This folder is organised into the subfolders below. Each subfolder has its own guide explaining what belongs in it.

- `conversations-and-dialogues`: Natural dialogues in any Cameroonian language or mixture of languages.
- `common-questions`: Questions Cameroonians commonly ask, with accurate local answers.
- `emergency-and-safety`: Emergency numbers, safety guidance and where to seek help, verified and dated.
