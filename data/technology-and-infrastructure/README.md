# Technology And Infrastructure

The systems that connect the country.

This folder is organised into the subfolders below. Each subfolder has its own guide explaining what belongs in it.

- `telecommunications`: Networks, operators, coverage and regulations.
- `energy-and-water`: Electricity, water supply and related institutions.
- `transport-and-roads`: Roads, railways, ports, airports and public transport.
- `digital-services`: E-government, online services and tech organisations.
