# Primary

Primary school certificates and entrance examinations in both systems.

This folder is organised into the subfolders below. Each subfolder has its own guide explaining what belongs in it.

- `fslc`: First School Leaving Certificate questions and answers.
- `cep`: Certificat d'Etudes Primaires questions and answers.
- `common-entrance`: Common Entrance examination questions and answers.
- `concours-entree-sixieme`: Entrance competition into form one on the Francophone side.
