# Francophone Secondary

The Francophone secondary examination system. Write questions in their original French.

This folder is organised into the subfolders below. Each subfolder has its own guide explaining what belongs in it.

- `bepc`: Brevet d'Etudes du Premier Cycle questions and corrections.
- `probatoire`: Probatoire questions and corrections, by series and subject.
- `baccalaureat`: Baccalauréat questions and corrections, by series and subject.
- `cap-bep`: CAP and BEP technical certificate questions and corrections.
- `baccalaureat-technique`: Technical Baccalauréat and Probatoire technique questions and corrections.
