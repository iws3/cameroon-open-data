# University Concours

Competitive entrance examinations for universities, grandes écoles and professional schools.

This folder is organised into the subfolders below. Each subfolder has its own guide explaining what belongs in it.

- `medicine-and-health`: Concours for medical, nursing, pharmacy and health science schools.
- `engineering-and-technology`: Concours for polytechnic, engineering and technology schools.
- `teacher-training`: Concours for teacher training colleges and higher teacher training schools.
- `public-administration-and-law`: Concours for administration, magistracy, finance and law schools.
- `agriculture-and-veterinary`: Concours for agriculture, forestry and veterinary schools.
- `security-services`: Recruitment and entrance competitions for the police, gendarmerie and armed forces.
- `other-concours`: Any other competitive entrance or recruitment examination.
