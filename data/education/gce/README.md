# Gce

The Anglophone General Certificate of Education system, run by the GCE Board.

This folder is organised into the subfolders below. Each subfolder has its own guide explaining what belongs in it.

- `ordinary-level`: GCE Ordinary Level past questions, marking guides and worked answers, by subject and year.
- `advanced-level`: GCE Advanced Level past questions, marking guides and worked answers, by subject and year.
- `technical-ordinary-level`: Technical and vocational GCE Ordinary Level papers, including practical and theory components.
- `technical-advanced-level`: Technical and vocational GCE Advanced Level papers, including practical and theory components.
- `other-gce-examinations`: Other examinations run by the GCE Board, such as professional and commercial certificates.
