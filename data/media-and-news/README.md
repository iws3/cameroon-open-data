# Media And News

How Cameroon talks about itself.

This folder is organised into the subfolders below. Each subfolder has its own guide explaining what belongs in it.

- `newspapers-and-magazines`: Articles you have the right to share, with full citation.
- `radio-and-television`: Transcripts of broadcasts, with the broadcaster named.
- `fact-checks`: Claims circulating in Cameroon and what the evidence shows.
