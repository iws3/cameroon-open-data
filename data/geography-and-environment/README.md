# Geography And Environment

The land, its divisions and its living things.

This folder is organised into the subfolders below. Each subfolder has its own guide explaining what belongs in it.

- `administrative-units`: Regions, divisions, subdivisions and councils.
- `places-and-place-names`: Towns, villages, landmarks and the meaning and origin of their names.
- `climate-and-weather`: Climate zones, seasons and weather records.
- `biodiversity-and-protected-areas`: Plants, animals, parks and reserves.
- `natural-resources`: Forests, water, minerals, oil and gas.
