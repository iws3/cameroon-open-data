# Law And Justice

The legal framework and the courts.

This folder is organised into the subfolders below. Each subfolder has its own guide explaining what belongs in it.

- `constitution-and-statutes`: The Constitution and published laws, in English and French.
- `ohada-and-regional-law`: OHADA and CEMAC legal texts that apply in Cameroon.
- `court-decisions`: Published court decisions with personal details removed.
- `legal-procedures`: Plain-language explanations of how legal procedures work.
- `rights-and-citizenship`: Citizens' rights, duties and civic education.
