# Religion And Community

Beliefs, associations and community life.

This folder is organised into the subfolders below. Each subfolder has its own guide explaining what belongs in it.

- `religions-and-beliefs`: Christian, Muslim, traditional and other beliefs and practices.
- `community-associations`: Tontines, njangi groups, hometown unions and other associations.
- `social-customs`: Greetings, etiquette, family structures and everyday customs.
