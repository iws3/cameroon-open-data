# Politics And Governance

How Cameroon is governed and how its public life works.

This folder is organised into the subfolders below. Each subfolder has its own guide explaining what belongs in it.

- `elections`: Electoral rules, official results and election procedures.
- `parliament`: The National Assembly and Senate, their rules and public proceedings.
- `political-parties`: Registered parties, their founding and their published platforms.
- `government-institutions`: Ministries, agencies, councils and their mandates.
- `decentralization`: Regions, councils and the laws and practice of decentralization.
- `speeches-and-communiques`: Public speeches, official communiqués and decrees.
- `public-administration`: How citizens deal with the administration: procedures, forms and requirements.
