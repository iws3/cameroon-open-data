# History

Where Cameroon and its peoples come from.

This folder is organised into the subfolders below. Each subfolder has its own guide explaining what belongs in it.

- `precolonial`: Kingdoms, migrations and societies before colonial rule.
- `colonial-period`: German, French and British administration and its effects.
- `independence-and-reunification`: The road to independence, the plebiscite and reunification.
- `post-independence`: The republic, its institutions and major events since independence.
- `oral-histories`: Recorded and written memories of elders and eyewitnesses, with consent.
- `archives-and-documents`: Historical documents that may be freely shared, with full citations.
