# Health

Health, care and wellbeing.

This folder is organised into the subfolders below. Each subfolder has its own guide explaining what belongs in it.

- `public-health`: Disease patterns, campaigns and national health programmes.
- `health-facilities`: Hospitals, clinics and health districts.
- `medical-terms-in-local-languages`: Health vocabulary in Cameroonian languages and Pidgin.
- `health-education`: Plain-language health guidance written for ordinary people.
