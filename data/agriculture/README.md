# Agriculture

Farming, fishing and the food chain.

This folder is organised into the subfolders below. Each subfolder has its own guide explaining what belongs in it.

- `crops`: Crops, varieties and growing practices by region.
- `livestock-and-fisheries`: Livestock, fishing and aquaculture practices.
- `farming-calendars`: Planting and harvest seasons by region.
- `markets-and-prices`: Market locations and dated price observations.
- `farmer-advice`: Practical questions and answers for farmers.
