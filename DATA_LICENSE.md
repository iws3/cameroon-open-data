# Data license

Data contributed to this repository is shared under the [Creative Commons Attribution-ShareAlike 4.0 International license](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-sa/4.0/). In plain words, anyone allowed to use the data may copy and build on it, including commercially, as long as they credit the contributors and share any adapted version under the same terms.

## What this means for contributors

You keep ownership of what you contribute. By opening a pull request you confirm that you wrote the material or have the right to share it, and you allow it to be shared under this license. Your name stays attached to your contributions in the history of the repository and in the `contributor` field of each record.

## Material you do not own

Some records describe or quote material that belongs to others, such as examination boards, authors or broadcasters. Those records must carry the correct `rights` value in their metadata. A record marked `permission_granted` should have the permission described in its source citation. A record marked `unclear_needs_review` will be reviewed by maintainers and may be removed before any public release if the rights cannot be confirmed.

## Cultural knowledge

A license cannot capture everything. Some knowledge belongs to a community and carries responsibilities that go beyond copyright. Records marked `culturally_sensitive` or `needs_community_review` are treated according to the process in `docs/RIGHTS_AND_PRIVACY.md`, and members of the relevant community have the final say about whether they are released.
