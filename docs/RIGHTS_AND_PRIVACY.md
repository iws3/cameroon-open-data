# Rights, privacy and cultural respect

Collecting data is a responsibility. This guide explains how to protect the people and the institutions behind it.

## Ownership and copyright

Examination papers are produced by examination bodies and are normally protected by copyright. Books, newspaper articles, songs and broadcasts belong to their authors and publishers. Being able to find something on the internet does not mean it is free to copy.

Material that is safest to contribute includes questions and answers you wrote yourself, teaching notes you own, texts that a body has published for the public to reuse, laws and official government documents that are published openly, anything in the public domain, and traditional oral knowledge that you are sharing with the consent of your community. When you are not sure, mark the record `unclear_needs_review`, or ask the owner for written permission and describe that permission in the citation. Maintainers will help.

Copies of papers that circulate on websites or in messaging groups often carry adverts, phone numbers or a site watermark. Those belong to whoever added them and are not part of the examination. Type or transcribe only the questions themselves, and leave out the adverts and contact details. A page that is a photograph of someone else's compiled material is not made free to share just because it is online.

## Privacy of living people

Never include private phone numbers, personal email addresses, home addresses, national identity numbers, bank or mobile money details, or medical information about a named person. Remove the names of private individuals from court decisions, news stories and testimonies unless they are public figures acting in a public role. The validator warns when it spots phone numbers or email addresses, but it cannot catch everything, so please check your own work.

## Recordings of people

Record only people who understand what the recording is for and who agree to it being shared, preferably in writing or on the recording itself. Set `consent_of_speakers` to true only when every speaker has agreed. Children must have the consent of a parent or guardian.

## Cultural and community knowledge

Some stories, songs, rites and medicines are not meant for everyone. If you are not sure whether something may be shared, mark it `culturally_sensitive` or `needs_community_review`, and describe the concern in your pull request. Records with these marks are held back from any wider release until members of the relevant community agree.

## Politics

Political material is welcome when it is accurate, sourced and attributed. We record what was said, decided or published, by whom and when. We do not publish rumours or accusations against named people that are not supported by a reliable source.

## If you made a mistake

If you realise that you shared something private or something you did not have the right to share, tell a maintainer straight away. We will remove it from the current files and from the history where possible, and no one will blame you for acting honestly.
