# Easy start: contribute without any technical skill

If you can read a question and type it, you can contribute. Choose the way that suits you.

## Before you start

If you plan to type a whole paper, open "Claim a paper to transcribe" in the Issues tab first, and check that nobody else already holds it. For a single question you can go straight to the form. If the question is already in the collection, the system will tell you kindly and nothing is lost.

## What can I share?

Cameroon is far more than its exams. In the Issues tab, under New issue, you will find a form for each kind of contribution:

- **Submit an exam question**: GCE, BEPC, Probatoire, Baccalauréat, concours and other examination questions.
- **Submit a text**: a proverb, a folktale, a speech, a law, a recipe, a description of a custom, an oral history.
- **Submit a question and its answer**: something people in Cameroon ask, with an answer you know is right.
- **Submit a translation**: the same sentence in two languages, such as Pidgin and English, or Ewondo and French.
- **Submit facts about a place, institution, law, crop or event**: for example a town, a ministry, a festival, a football club.
- **Share a recording, scan or document**: audio, video or a scan that a volunteer will transcribe.

Each form is a set of ordinary boxes. Choose the folder that fits, and if you are not sure, choose the last option and the maintainers will place it for you. Every form asks where your contribution comes from and whether you have the right to share it. "I am not sure" is always an acceptable answer.

## Way 1: the exam question form (about five minutes per question)

1. Open the repository on GitHub and click the Issues tab.
2. Click New issue, then choose "Submit an exam question".
3. Fill in the boxes. Choose the exam, write the subject, and type the whole question with all its parts and marks. If you do not know a box, leave it empty.
4. Choose honestly where the question came from and whether you have the right to share it. "I am not sure" is a good answer.
5. Tick the agreement box and press Submit new issue.

Within a minute the system turns your answers into a record and sends it to a reviewer. You will get a reply on your issue.

## Way 2: photos of a paper (no typing)

Choose "Share photos of a paper" instead. Select the exam and subject, drag your pictures into the box and submit. Good pictures make transcription possible: bright light, paper flat on a table, one page per picture, every line readable.

## Way 3: a spreadsheet (for many questions)

Download `templates/exam-questions-template.csv`, open it in Excel or Google Sheets, put one question per row, save it as CSV, and send it through "Send a spreadsheet of questions". The column guide is in `templates/README.md`.

## Tips for typing a question

- Type exactly what is printed, including spelling.
- Keep all parts of one numbered question together.
- Keep the marks where the paper prints them, for example (2 marks).
- Describe any diagram in words.
- Write [unclear] if a word cannot be read.
- Leave out adverts, website names and phone numbers.
- Write formulas in plain text, for example m s^-1 or 2x + 3 = 11.

## Reviewing is a contribution too

If you know a subject well, open the pull requests from submitted questions and check the typing and the answers. Your name is added as a verifier when you approve.
