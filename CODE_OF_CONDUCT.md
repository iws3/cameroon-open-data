# Code of conduct

This project belongs to everyone from every region, language, religion, ethnic group, political view and generation in Cameroon. We commit to making it a safe and welcoming place for all of them.

## What we expect

Treat other members the way you would want to be treated in your own village or your own family. Listen before you correct. Credit the work of others. Assume that a mistake is a mistake and not an insult. When you disagree, talk about the data and the evidence, not about the person.

## What we do not accept

We do not accept insults, threats, harassment, or demeaning comments about anyone's tribe, region, language, religion, gender, disability or politics. We do not accept anglophone versus francophone point scoring, and we do not accept posting private information about other people. Political material is welcome as data when it is recorded accurately and attributed. It is not welcome as a weapon against fellow members.

## What happens when someone breaks these rules

Maintainers will explain the problem in private first. Serious or repeated violations lead to removal from the project, as described in `ACCESS.md`. Anyone can report a concern to a maintainer, and reports are treated confidentially.
